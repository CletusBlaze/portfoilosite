Frequently Asked

Common questions answered

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 2-4 weeks in advance for portrait sessions and 3-6 months for weddings.

How long until I receive my photos?

Portrait sessions are delivered within 1-2 days. Weddings and events take 1 week for full gallery delivery.

Do you travel for shoots?

Yes! We're available for destination shoots worldwide. Travel fees may apply depending on location.

What should I wear to my session?

We provide a style guide upon booking. Generally, solid colors and timeless outfits photograph best.
