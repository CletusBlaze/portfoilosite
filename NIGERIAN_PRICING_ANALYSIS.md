## FACTORS TO CONSIDER

### Benin City Market Specifics:
1. **Lower cost of living** than Lagos/Abuja
2. **Different purchasing power** than major cities
3. **Competition level** with local photographers
4. **Target clientele** (middle class vs luxury market)

### Your Competitive Advantages:
1. **Fast delivery** (1-2 days portraits, 1 week weddings)
2. **Professional quality** equipment and editing
3. **Comprehensive packages** 
4. **Nigerian cultural understanding**

## RECOMMENDATIONS

### 1. Research Local Competition
- Check Instagram/Facebook for Benin City photographers
- Look at their pricing (often posted in stories/highlights)
- Note their service offerings
- Identify gaps you can fill

### 2. Consider Market Positioning
- **Premium positioning**: Keep higher prices, emphasize quality/speed
- **Competitive positioning**: Adjust prices to match local market
- **Value positioning**: Moderate prices with exceptional service

### 3. Test Market Response
- Launch with current pricing
- Monitor booking inquiries
- Adjust based on client feedback
- Offer promotional packages initially

### 4. Add Nigerian-Specific Packages
- **Traditional Wedding**: Separate package for traditional ceremonies
- **Naming Ceremony**: Baby naming ceremony photography
- **Graduation**: University graduation packages
