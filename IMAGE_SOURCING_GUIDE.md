## DOWNLOAD INSTRUCTIONS

### Step 1: Visit the recommended sites
1. Go to Unsplash.com or Pexels.com
2. Use the search terms provided above
3. Look for images that match the descriptions

### Step 2: Download Process
1. Click on the image you want
2. Click "Free Download" button
3. Choose "Small" size (usually sufficient for testimonials)
4. Save to your computer

### Step 3: Rename and Place Files
1. Rename downloaded files to:
   - testimonials-1.jpg
   - testimonials-2.jpg
   - testimonials-3.jpg
   - testimonials-4.jpg
   - testimonials-5.jpg

2. Replace existing files in folder:
   `images/testimonials/`

## IMAGE SPECIFICATIONS
- **Minimum size:** 120px x 120px
- **Recommended:** 300px x 300px or larger
- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Quality:** High resolution, clear faces
- **Aspect ratio:** Square or portrait works best
